# Biome preview

A preview of the Biome landing page: business sentences pour into an ocean made of their own text, and a humpback breaches through it. Text layout by [Pretext](https://github.com/chenglou/pretext), drawn with WebGL.

- [Landing page](https://sabeel111.github.io/biome-preview/)
- [Sunset variant](https://sabeel111.github.io/biome-preview/sunset.html)
- [The animation on its own](https://sabeel111.github.io/biome-preview/ocean.html)

Buttons are not connected to anything yet.
