How it works

Ask it anything. Let it watch everything.

Biome is not a chat box over your data. It is an agent that knows your business, answers when you ask, and speaks up when you don't.

Ask

Ask in plain words

Get the figure, how it was worked out, and the data behind it. Follow up the way you would with your accountant.

What were net sales last month?
₹48.2 lakh▲ 11% on October
Net sales = invoices − credit notes, before GST

Watch

Tell it what matters

Describe a rule in a sentence. Biome tests it against your history, then keeps watch, day and night.

Tell me if debtors over 90 days pass ₹5 lakh.
Watching · checked 4 min ago

Alert

Hear about it first

Why trust it

Defined, not guessed.

Biome doesn't write a fresh query every time you ask. It answers from metrics defined for your business, so the same question gets the same figure, every time.

  • Every answer shows its metric, its definition and the tables it came from.
  • Definitions come from you, written down once while Biome learns your business.
  • Checks run on every figure before you see it.
